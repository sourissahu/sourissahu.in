Battery Power Optimization for Dashboard

VLSIBMSARCI HyderabadMATLAB

Battery Health Monitoring for accurate and real-time Battery Management System (BMS) for pure and Hybrid Electric Vehicles, developed in collaboration with ARCI (Advanced Research Centre for Powder Metallurgy and New Materials), Hyderabad.

Designed and implemented algorithms for State of Charge (SoC) and State of Health (SoH) estimation, enabling a smart dashboard interface to display accurate, real-time battery metrics for EV applications.

The BMS incorporates fault detection, thermal management interfaces, and power optimization routines, significantly improving battery longevity and vehicle reliability in both pure electric and hybrid configurations.
