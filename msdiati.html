Ms.Diati

Your Personal AI Dietitian

Ms.Diati is an AI-powered nutrition and health companion designed to provide personalized nutrition guidance, food analysis, meal tracking and conversational health information.

Why does Ms.Diati connect to Cloudflare?

Ms.Diati can create a personal AI Worker in your own Cloudflare account. This allows your AI requests to use your own Cloudflare resources rather than sharing one central AI account with other users.

You remain in control of your Cloudflare account and its resources.

Privacy

Health and nutrition information can be sensitive. Ms.Diati is designed to minimize unnecessary sharing of personal information and to use information only when needed to provide personalized assistance.

Ms.Diati is an informational and nutrition-support tool. It does not replace a qualified doctor, registered dietitian, emergency service or other healthcare professional.

Cloudflare Authorization

When you choose to connect Cloudflare, you are redirected to Cloudflare to authenticate and authorize Ms.Diati. Ms.Diati does not ask you to provide your Cloudflare password or place a long-lived Cloudflare API token inside the mobile application.
